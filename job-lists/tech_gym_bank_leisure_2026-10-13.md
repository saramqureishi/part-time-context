## 🎬🎢 Leisure & Entertainment — ⚠️ MIXED (read the honest notes!)

### ⚠️ Cinemas — be careful (these involve FOOD/DRINK serving)
Vue, Cineworld, Odeon "Customer Assistant / Team Member" roles include **"serving at retail counters / food & beverage"** and **"keeping the cinema clean and tidy."** That's **serving + cleaning**, which goes against your no-serving/no-labour rule.
- ❌ **My honest advice: skip these** unless you're OK with popcorn/drinks counter service and clearing screens. The "host" part is mixed with serving.
- Careers (if you still want to look): Vue https://careers.myvue.com/ · Cineworld https://careers.cineworld.co.uk/jobs · Odeon https://careers.odeon.co.uk/

### ⚠️ Wembley Stadium / OVO Arena — mostly stewarding (labour/standing, event-based)
Most roles are **Matchday Steward / ticket checking / crowd management** — that's security/labour, not communication, and only on event days.
- ❌ **Skip stewarding.** ✅ **Exception:** "Events Coordinator / Visitor Experience / box office" admin roles if they appear — those are communication-based.
- OVO Arena: https://www.ovoarena.co.uk/your-visit/working-at-ovo-arena-wembley · Wembley/events: https://uk.indeed.com/q-stadium-events-l-wembley-jobs.html

### ⚠️ Trampoline parks / soft play — often labour + safety marshalling
These tend to be active floor-marshalling/supervising roles (on your feet, physical). Reception/party-host desk roles can be OK, but check carefully.

### 🏛️ Museums / heritage — ✅ GOOD if customer-facing (Visitor Experience / Front of House)
These suit you well when they're "Visitor Experience Assistant / Front of House" (welcoming, info, tickets). Check: National Trust https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/jobs · English Heritage https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/jobs
